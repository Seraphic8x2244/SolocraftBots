## Boss Mods — consolidated design contract and research (accepted 2026-10-13–15)

### Scope, ownership and files
- Boss Mods is a small, optional **module inside SoloCraftBots**, not a standalone addon and not a dependency on BigWigs. Build reusable primitives instead of porting a large boss-mod framework. Later reuse should extend to AQ40, Naxxramas and other raids without bespoke engines.
- **Two deliberate extra root Lua files:** `mechanics.lua` is the generic engine/framework; `encounters.lua` is exclusively **declarative Lua-table definitions** (ordinary Lua 5.0.3 tables, not a new configuration language, parser or individual boss functions). Encounter content supplies zone/boss detection, event triggers, assets, labels, durations, named bindings, primitive types, and action sequences. Adding an encounter should normally require **only** editing `encounters.lua`. New mechanics belong in the engine only when truly reusable.
- Both agreed root files are now present on `dev`: `mechanics.lua` is the dormant generic engine and `encounters.lua` holds editable Lua tables for eight BWL bosses. ### Operational caveats and source-to-SCB translation
1. **Separate fact from inference:** Observed chat/emote/aura events are evidence; intervals and HP ETAs are estimates; target disappearance and GUID-neighbour deductions are inference. Mark visualisation accordingly. Reset phase/timers on real observed transitions, clean teardown/generation changes, wipe/death/zone changes. Do **not** synthesize unavailable abilities (especially Chromaggus 0/1/2 breaths).
2. **Duplicate NPC identity:** Prefer Rupturan-style GUID exclusion; all add/boss HP tracking requires a valid visible unit route. Test actual SCB-supported client capabilities and fallback paths; bot roster != observable NPC list.
3. **Combat-log completeness:** Viscidus hit counters require combat events from *bots*, not just human raid members. Log strings and localization/server variations may differ. Show unknown/approximate instead of misleading precision.
4. **Command/safety ownership:** Reuse SCB bot identity, `SCB_RequestCommand` and `SCB_KickBots` paths. After a Boss Mods addon-controlled target swap, settle 0.10s by default inside `mechanics.lua`. 5. **Timing/provenance:** Fixed source values (Ouro submerge; C’Thun Dark Glare; etc.) are not guaranteed on Solocraft, and their failures are visible in the Lua itself. Prefer observed-event correction to autonomous clock assumptions.
6. **TimeToKill / time-to-threshold:** user suggested “TimeToKill”; research found ItsFovv `TimeToKill` (Classic 1.13.4, 2020) and dkff7chicken `Time To Kill` (Classic 1.15.6, 2025) described as **linear regression**; **their Lua has NOT been source-audited or shown 1.12.1 compatible**. Require enough samples; invalidate/suppress on heal/stall, phase reset, GUID change, loss of visibility, or nonpositive slope; label estimate. Additional comparable research reference: `Broker: TimeToExecute` (rolling historical HP and configurable execute%). **No new estimator approved/implemented yet**.

## Boss Mods engine refactor — agreed architecture before initial BWL raid test (2026-10-19)
- **User-approved sequence:** finish BWL boss-by-boss specification first; then perform one robust refactor of `mechanics.lua` and the declarative `encounters.lua` schema **before** enabling real Boss Mods encounters and the user's initial BWL raid test. - **Current implementation limitation:** `mechanics.lua` presently implements encounter-scoped named timers, event→effect dispatch, separately rendered aura rows and clickable bot actions. It **does not** implement linked per-NPC ability records, cast occurrences, cast-to-aura parentage, cast blocking, persistent ready/casting state or filling bars. Treat the current event effects as source material to migrate, not as proof of the refactored engine. No parallel scheduler, shadow roster or independent OnUpdate; retain SCB-owned lifecycle, roster, event and command routing.
- **Canonical ownership:** `Encounter → caster NPC identity → ability definition → cast occurrence → zero/more aura instances per recipient`. Resolve caster by GUID where the current environment provides a reliable one; otherwise use a verified unique unit/name, with ambiguity handled explicitly. A caster's ordinary-cast lock affects **only that NPC**, not other NPCs in the encounter (e.g. AQ40 Bug Trio). Per-caster lock applies even to ordinary instant casts as relevant; explicit triggered/scripted bypasses require evidence. Merely being script-driven does not prove immunity to cast locking. Distinct bosses can cast simultaneously.
- **Declarative ability fields (proposed grammar):** unique `id`, caster selector, timer behaviour `fixed` / `priority` / `opportunistic`, time-to-first and its start trigger (usually observed engage), time-between and **the event it counts from**, predicted/observed provenance, cast spell ID/name observation alternatives, expected cast length, cast-start observation, cast-finish observation, and **separate** timer-reset observation. Spell IDs and GUIDs are optional when Vanilla logs do not supply them; match source, target and caster conservatively. Exact spelling/names of fields to settle once during refactor, not per boss. Don't conflate spell-hit, cast-end and next-cooldown start.
- **Ability modes:** `fixed` = confirmed scheduled mechanic with its own trigger/execution and explicitly defined cast-lock exemption if any; `priority` = ready at X and expected at first permitted opportunity; `opportunistic` = ready at X and may execute later. These are UI/engine prediction semantics, **not guaranteed categories of actual WoW scripting**; don't classify a boss ability as priority/opportunistic solely because BigWigs supplies a bar. Do not fabricate casts at expected timestamps.
- **Bar visual standard (user corrected and confirmed):** bar **fills** from empty to 100%, BLUE while 100–25% cooldown remains, AMBER once under 25% remains down to readiness, then **100% filled SOLID GREEN** while ready, including if its caster is occupied casting another spell. On **observed cast start** switch to **FULL FLASHING GREEN** during the actual cast; on the configured completion/reset observation start a fresh fill according to that ability. **Flashing green means CASTING, never merely READY**. The *bar fill*, not its border, changes colour/flashes. Readiness stays solid green through other spells' casts; another cast does not extend/reset its cooldown.
- **Cast observations and lifecycle:** Start/finish must be distinguishable. A hit, aura application or cast-finish event may close a cast when configured and truly observed. A single AoE cast can emit multiple hit messages; deduplicate all recipients to one cast completion/cooldown reset. Support missing or delayed cast-start logs: an observed hit/aura can still record the event without inventing the missing preceding cast. If an expected finish is absent, retain an **uncertain/unresolved** outcome rather than treating elapsed expected cast length as confirmed success. Separately handle interruption/failure/cancellation and encounter reset. Predicted cast duration can be used for display but is not authoritative server completion.
- **Linked auras:** Define `cast` and zero/more `auras` **beneath the same parent ability**. A spellcast and its resulting aura may have different spell IDs, target selection, duration and gain/fade observations. Each recipient aura owns its individual apply/refresh/remove/death/despawn lifecycle and optionally an individual action (Vael filtered kick), independent of other recipients and of parent cooldown reset. An observed aura still appears even when no cast-start message was seen; association with a particular cast occurrence may be best-effort. Aura gain can be the configured completion/reset observation if warranted, but is not automatically so. Reuse existing SCB live-roster/death/filtered-kick cleanup.
- **Runtime instances:** Maintain encounter generation; caster identity and current ordinary cast lock; for each ability its readiness state and timestamps (`startedAt`, `readyAt`, actual cast start, expected cast end, last observed/reset), linked cast occurrence ID, and associated per-recipient aura instances. ## Architecture / ownership
- `SoloCraftBots.lua`: bootstrap/core/shared UI/primitives.
- `Presets.lua`: configured preset intent, editor, snapshots, validation and raid-role tracker creation/finalization.
- `Roster.lua`: observed party/raid reality, human/bot classification, Active Roster, provisional spawn assumptions, combat-role evidence and passive layout classification.
- `Spawn.lua`: sole owner of physical roster mutation through `SCB.botOperation`: summon/rebuild, add, subgroup moves, maintenance/refill, bootstrap/survivor handling.
- `Communication.lua`: PartyBot command transport, command semantics, incoming chat/system feedback and preset communications.
- `Options.lua`: settings/help/debug.
- Preserve one physical-operation coordinator. Presentation code must not mutate roster state.

## Authoritative logical / physical model
- A preset slot is intent. A human assigned to logical slot N suppresses exactly that slot's bot class/role intent.
- Party physical order is client-relative and cannot represent shared logical identity. Never warn because a party human is not in their logical absolute row.
- Raid subgroup membership is enforceable with `SetRaidSubgroup()`; exact row/order inside a subgroup is not.
- During raid rebuild, configured humans must be moved to their intended subgroup by name before bot bursts continue. Re-resolve raid indices immediately before each move.
- Human absolute row is irrelevant to bot identity.
- Full rebuilds intentionally burst one logical group at a time. Human-covered assignments are omitted, so a group with two humans and three bot intents summons exactly three bots.
- Preserve the established reverse-send/LIFO behaviour and the existing per-group settle boundary. Do not pack full-rebuild assignments across groups merely to save a burst.
- Final identity is the settled subgroup's **bot-only ordinal order** mapped to uncovered logical assignments in ascending slot order. This is the proven pre-0.8.87 principle from `6bcc9949222513d4f8e38a90d43071149f162f31`.
- Join-line name -> intent binding is provisional/operational identity. It remains important for mixed-group refill, but it must not overwrite settled full-rebuild ordinal identity.
